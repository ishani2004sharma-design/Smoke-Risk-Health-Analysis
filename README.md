# 🚬 Smoke Risk Health Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

Smoke Risk Health Analysis is a data analytics project that explores the relationship between smoking habits, health conditions, and associated risk factors. The project focuses on analyzing health-related data to identify patterns, understand the impact of smoking on individuals, and generate meaningful insights through data visualization.

By using data analysis techniques and interactive dashboards, this project aims to present complex health information in a simple and understandable format.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze smoking habits and their relationship with health indicators.
* Identify patterns and trends in smoking-related health risks.
* Explore the distribution of health conditions across different groups.
* Understand the impact of various risk factors on health.
* Create meaningful visualizations to support data-driven insights.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **SQL:** Data querying, filtering, and analysis.
* **Power BI:** Interactive dashboards and data visualization.
* **Microsoft Excel:** Data organization and preprocessing.

## 📊 Key Analysis Areas

* Smoking habits and their distribution.
* Health risk factor analysis.
* Comparison of health indicators among different groups.
* Identification of patterns in smoking-related health conditions.
* Visualization of key metrics and trends.

## 💡 Key Insights

The analysis helps explore how smoking habits are associated with different health indicators and risk factors. Visualizations make it easier to identify trends, compare groups, and understand relationships within the dataset.
